Parcel Delivery: Customer Help Guide

Policies and how-to answers for senders, receivers and couriers using the Parcel Delivery service.

What is the Parcel Delivery service and who uses it?

Parcel Delivery is a courier service. A sender books a parcel, an admin assigns a courier to it, the courier carries it through each delivery stage, and the receiver tracks it and confirms when it arrives.

There are four kinds of account: sender, receiver, delivery personnel (courier) and admin. A sender books and pays for parcels. A receiver is the person a parcel is addressed to. A courier picks up and delivers parcels assigned to them. An admin manages users, assigns couriers and can put a parcel on hold.

How do I create an account?

Sign up with your name, email address and a password. When you sign up you choose a role: sender, receiver or delivery personnel. If you do not choose, the account is created as a sender. Admin accounts cannot be created by signing up; only an existing admin can create another admin.

Each email address can have only one account. If the email is already registered, sign-up is refused and you should sign in or reset your password instead. A national ID number can also be linked to only one account.

What are the password requirements?

A password must be at least 8 characters long. The same minimum applies when you sign up, change your password, or reset a forgotten password.

How do I verify my email address?

After you sign up, a confirmation email is sent with a link. The confirmation link is valid for 24 hours. If the link has expired or you cannot find the email, ask for a new confirmation email from your account and use the newest link. You can sign in and use your account before your email is verified.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email address. If an account exists for that address, a password reset link is emailed to you. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If it has expired, request a new one.

For privacy, the page shows the same message whether or not the email address is registered. Resetting your password also marks your email address as verified, and it signs you out on every device.

How long do I stay signed in?

You stay signed in as long as you use the service at least once every 7 days. After 7 days without activity you are asked to sign in again. Changing or resetting your password ends every session on every device, so you will need to sign in again everywhere. You can also sign out of one device, or sign out of all devices at once.

How do I apply to become a courier (delivery personnel)?

Sign up and choose the delivery personnel role. Your account is created as a pending courier application. While the application is pending you can sign in and see your profile, but you cannot be assigned parcels or use the courier screens yet. An admin reviews the application, and once it is approved your account becomes delivery personnel and parcels can be assigned to you.

How do I send (book) a parcel?

Only a sender account can book a parcel. To book one you provide the receiver's name, the receiver's email address, the pickup address and the delivery address. You may also add the receiver's phone number, a description such as "Fragile, handle with care", the parcel weight in kilograms, and a cash on delivery amount.

If you leave out the weight, the parcel is treated as 1 kilogram. The weight must be between 0.01 kg and 1000 kg. If you leave out the cash on delivery amount, the parcel is prepaid. A new parcel starts in the Pending status and receives a tracking ID.

Can I send a parcel to someone who does not have an account?

Yes. Enter the receiver's email address when you book. If nobody has registered with that email, an account is created for the receiver automatically and they are emailed a "claim your account" link to set a password. The claim link is valid for 7 days. If it expires, the receiver can use "Forgot password" with the same email address to get a new link.

Can I send a parcel to myself, or to a blocked account?

No. You cannot book a parcel where you are both the sender and the receiver. A booking is also refused if the receiver's account has been blocked or deleted; in that case use a different receiver email address.

How much does delivery cost? How is the delivery fee calculated?

The delivery fee is calculated by the service from the parcel's weight and its cash on delivery amount. The base fee is 60 and covers the first kilogram. Each additional kilogram costs 25, and a part kilogram is rounded up to a full kilogram. If the parcel is cash on delivery, a handling fee of 1% of the cash amount is added. The minimum delivery fee is 60.

Examples: a prepaid parcel of 1 kg or less costs 60. A prepaid parcel of 2.3 kg costs 110 (60 base plus 2 extra kilograms at 25 each). A 3 kg parcel with 2000 cash on delivery costs 130 (60 base, 50 for weight, 20 cash handling fee).

What does a tracking ID look like, and how do I track a parcel?

Every parcel has a tracking ID that starts with TRK- followed by 12 letters and digits, for example TRK-7K2M9QX4T1VB. Parcels booked earlier may have a longer tracking ID; those still work.

Anyone who has the tracking ID can track the parcel on the tracking page without signing in. The tracking page shows the current status, the sender's and receiver's names, the pickup and delivery addresses, the description, the first name of the courier, and the history of status changes with the time of each one.

What do the parcel statuses mean?

Pending: the parcel has been booked and is waiting for a courier to collect it. Picked up: the courier has collected the parcel from the pickup address. In transit: the parcel is travelling towards the delivery area. Out for delivery: the courier is bringing the parcel to the delivery address. Delivered: the parcel has been handed over. Cancelled: the delivery was stopped and will not be completed.

A parcel normally moves in this order: Pending, Picked up, In transit, Out for delivery, Delivered. On some routes a parcel goes straight from In transit to Delivered. Statuses cannot be skipped otherwise and cannot move backwards. Delivered and Cancelled are final and cannot be changed.

What emails and notifications will I get about my parcel?

Both the sender and the receiver are emailed when the parcel is picked up, when it is out for delivery, when it is delivered and when it is cancelled. Each email contains the tracking ID, the courier's name if one is assigned, and a link to the tracking page. No email is sent for the In transit status.

If you are signed in, you also get live notifications in the app when your parcel changes status, when a courier is assigned or removed, and when a parcel is put on hold or released.

Can I cancel a parcel? What is the cancellation policy?

A sender can cancel their own parcel only while it is still Pending, which means before the courier has picked it up. You can only cancel parcels that you booked yourself.

Once a parcel has been picked up, the sender can no longer cancel it. Contact support, and an admin can cancel it for you as long as it has not been delivered yet. A delivered parcel cannot be cancelled, and a cancelled parcel cannot be reopened; book a new parcel instead.

How does cash on delivery (COD) work?

With cash on delivery, the sender sets an amount when booking and the courier collects that cash from the receiver at handover. The amount can be anything up to 1,000,000. A parcel with no cash on delivery amount is prepaid and the receiver pays nothing at the door.

A cash on delivery parcel can only be completed by the courier submitting proof of delivery and recording that the cash was collected. Until the cash is recorded, the parcel cannot be marked Delivered and the receiver cannot confirm delivery. The sender pays a handling fee of 1% of the cash amount as part of the delivery fee.

I am receiving a parcel. How do I see it and confirm delivery?

Sign in with the email address the parcel was sent to. The Incoming list shows parcels that are on their way to you, and Delivery history shows parcels already delivered to you. These lists are available to every signed-in user, including senders and couriers, because anyone can be the receiver of a parcel.

When the parcel arrives you can confirm delivery, which marks it Delivered. You can only confirm a parcel addressed to you. For a cash on delivery parcel, the courier records the handover and the cash first, so you cannot confirm it yourself before that.

What is proof of delivery?

At handover the courier submits proof of delivery: between one and five photos or signature images. The courier can also record who actually took the parcel if it was not the named receiver, for example a neighbour or building security, and add a short note. Submitting proof marks the parcel Delivered and records the delivery time.

Why is my parcel on hold (blocked)?

An admin can put a parcel on hold. While a parcel is on hold nothing about it can change: its status cannot be updated, a courier cannot be assigned, it cannot be cancelled, and delivery cannot be confirmed or proven. The tracking page shows that the parcel is on hold. Only an admin can release it; when they do, the parcel continues from the status it had. If your parcel is on hold, contact support.

What can a courier do with a parcel?

An admin assigns each parcel to an approved courier, and can remove or change the courier before the parcel is delivered or cancelled. A courier can only update parcels assigned to them. A courier can mark a parcel Picked up, In transit, Out for delivery or Delivered, and can submit proof of delivery. A courier cannot cancel a parcel or put it on hold.

Why can't I sign in? What happens if my account is blocked?

If the email or password is wrong, sign-in fails with "Invalid email or password". If an admin has blocked the account, or the account has been deleted, sign-in is refused with a message saying so.

When an account is blocked, the user is signed out on every device immediately and cannot sign in until an admin unblocks it. New parcels cannot be booked to a blocked receiver. If you believe your account was blocked by mistake, contact support.

What can the assistant help with?

The assistant answers questions about how the Parcel Delivery service works, using this guide, and questions about parcels. You must be signed in to use it. Ask about a parcel by its tracking ID to get its current status, route and last update.

For privacy, the assistant only knows about parcels you sent, are receiving, or are delivering. If you ask about somebody else's parcel it will say it does not have that information. Admins can ask about any parcel.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact form on the website. Give your name, your email address, a topic and your message (10 to 2000 characters), and include the tracking ID if your question is about a parcel. Contact support to cancel a parcel that has already been picked up, to ask about a parcel that is on hold, or if you think your account was blocked by mistake.
